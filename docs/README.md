## Systems

The [Systems Index](systems/README.md) lists every game system, in these areas:

- [Golf Play](systems/golf-play/shot-accuracy.md): shots, balls, putts and AI.
- [Course Design](systems/course-design/hole-creation.md): holes, terrain, buildings, themes and tees.
- [Economy and Land](systems/economy-and-land/economy.md): money, land, staff and the world map.
- [Environment and Time](systems/environment-and-time/game-calendar.md): calendar, seasons, weather and wind.
- [Events](systems/events/event-feed.md): the event feed and tournaments.
- [Golfers](systems/golfers/golfer-spawning.md): arrivals, needs and satisfaction.
- [Ratings and Reputation](systems/ratings-and-reputation/course-rating.md): star ratings and reputation.
